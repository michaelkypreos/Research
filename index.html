Personal Profile

I am an independent philosopher focused on the intersection of biological constraints and normative behavior. My work integrates insights from thermodynamics, information theory, and complex systems to redefine agency and value.

Currently, I am developing Kerdotism—a gain-based theory of agency that bridges the gap between negentropy and moral norms.
